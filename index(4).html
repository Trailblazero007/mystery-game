Day One: Hong Kong

Dio's trail has gone cold in Hong Kong. Reports from the harbor may be a trap, and the Joestar group must separate real clues from rumors.

Your goal is to find a reliable lead to Dio's next destination by collecting three clues.

What Will You Do?

Choice One: Investigate the Market

Search the market stalls for another copy of the harbor symbol. Interview two shopkeepers and compare their stories.

Choice Two: Pursue the Figure

Follow the suspicious figure through the side streets. Be careful, because rushing ahead may lead to an ambush.
